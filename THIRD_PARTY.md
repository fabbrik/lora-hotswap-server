## What is committed here

Everything in this repository is original work under the MIT license. In particular the test
fixtures under `tests/LoraHotswap.Tests/fixtures/` are **synthetic**: generated by the seeded
scripts next to them (`make_*_fixtures.py`) with real PEFT, not copied from any published adapter.
No third-party weights are committed.
